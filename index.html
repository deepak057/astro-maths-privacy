Astro Maths – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Astro Maths is a maths practice app for children in Class 1 to Class 5. We take children's privacy seriously. The app does not collect, send or share any personal information.

What stays on the device

A player's name, picture, level progress, streak and scores are saved only on the device the app is installed on. They are never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or log-ins.
  • No ads and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo access.
  • No in-app purchases and no links that lead children out of the app.

Read-aloud feature

The "Listen" button uses the text-to-speech voice already built into the Android device. The question text is spoken by the device and is not sent to us.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, no personal information from children is collected, used or shared.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
